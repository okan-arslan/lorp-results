# Third-Party Materials

IBM ILOG CPLEX is proprietary, separately licensed software. Its Java library,
native libraries and license files are not included.

The Akca and Prodhon benchmark inputs and the expanded networks derived for
Arslan (2021) are included with the maintainer's confirmed redistribution
permission. Their existing third-party rights are retained; the software and
results licenses do not grant new rights over these inputs. The original
benchmark archive did not include an explicit benchmark license.

References: Akca, Berger and Ralphs (2009), *A branch-and-price algorithm for
combined location and routing problems under capacity restrictions*, in
*Operations Research and Cyber-Infrastructure*, pp. 309-330; Prodhon's
capacitated location-routing benchmark collection; Arslan (2021),
DOI 10.1016/j.trb.2021.02.010.
